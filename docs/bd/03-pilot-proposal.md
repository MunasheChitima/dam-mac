# Canva/Pexels product proof

## Goal

Show Canva the product we have already built, in their market terms:

- customers creating from assets they already have
- less unnecessary generation
- a stronger offering around owned and customer libraries

This is not a plug-in trial. It is a demonstration that the product already
does work Canva's market is moving toward.

## Thesis

The product was naturally heading into Canva's space.

Once existing assets are easy to find and reuse, people do not need to
generate as much. They enter content creation from their own library, or
from Canva's. That is the value. We would rather Canva have it directly.

## Proof shape

Show the product against a narrow, representative content slice:

- a curated set of Pexels assets
- a customer-like enterprise asset library
- associated campaign files, brand assets, and supporting documents

Keep it small enough to evaluate quickly, and real enough that they can see
the customer value without imagining a future integration.

## Example corpus

A practical proof corpus could include:

- 10,000 to 50,000 image and video assets from a selected Pexels subset
- a sample brand library with logos, product imagery, campaign files,
  templates, and supporting documents
- a prompt set drawn from realistic "I need something for this" workflows

## What to show

1. "Find me something like the hero image from our summer campaign."
2. "Give me an approved product image for a social post about [theme]."
3. "Start from our own assets instead of generating a new one."
4. "Find a Pexels asset that fits the look of this campaign."
5. "Show the best existing asset across our library and Canva-owned stock."

The point of each scenario is the same: the customer did not need to generate
first. They needed a better way into content they already had.

## Success metrics

### Primary metrics

- **retrieval hit rate**: how often an acceptable existing asset is found
  before generation is needed
- **avoided generation rate**: share of requests that would otherwise have
  gone to generation
- **asset reuse rate**: increase in reuse of customer-owned or Canva-owned
  assets

### Secondary metrics

- time to a satisfactory first result
- whether the user can start creating from their own library
- how often generation is still the right next step

### Economic metrics

- estimated avoided generation calls
- estimated reduction in AI spend on those workflows
- increased use of inventory Canva already owns or already hosts for
  customers

## Evaluation method

For each prompt in the test set:

1. show the current Canva-like path: search, then generate
2. show the product path: enter from existing assets first
3. record whether an acceptable asset was found without generation
4. record when generation was still the better answer

At the end, summarize:

- where creating from existing assets clearly wins
- where generation remains necessary
- what Canva's customers would get if this value lived in their product

## How to offer it

Do not offer a connector or a sandbox integration.

Offer the product itself:

> We have already built this. It was heading into your market. We would
> rather show you the value directly, on a representative content slice, and
> see whether it belongs with you.

## If they want it

If the proof is positive, the conversation can move to how Canva takes the
value on:

- bringing the product into Canva's offering
- giving enterprise customers a stronger way to create from their own assets
- using it against Pexels and customer libraries Canva already has
- commercial terms that follow from that, not from a plugin relationship

Do not lead with acquisition language. Lead with the product, the overlap,
and the preference not to compete.
